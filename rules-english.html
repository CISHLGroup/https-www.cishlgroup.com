Group's Rules


Basic Rules


1. Do not use profanity towards us, do not mention us when using profanity, do not mention our names when using profanity
2. Do not engage in abstract discussions that are morally wrong; if you want to be abstract, do so with a correct moral perspective
3. Must comply with the laws and regulations of our country (here "our country" refers to the People's Republic of China), and must not engage in any illegal activities


Special Rules


Art Rules


1. Do not draw any content that contains profanity
2. Do not draw any content that is morally wrong or abstract in a negative way
3. Do not draw any content that violates the laws and regulations of our country (the People's Republic of China)

International Communication Rules

1. Do not use profanity in international communication, do not mention us when using profanity, do not mention our names when using profanity
2. Do not engage in abstract discussions that are morally wrong in international communication; if you want to be abstract, do so with a correct moral perspective
3. People from different countries must comply with the laws and regulations of their respective countries and must not engage in any illegal activities
4. In international communication, must respect the culture and customs of other countries and must not make any discriminatory remarks

Programming Rules

1. Do not write any content that contains profanity
2. Do not write any content that is morally wrong or abstract in a negative way
3. Do not write any content that violates the laws and regulations of our country (the People's Republic of China)
